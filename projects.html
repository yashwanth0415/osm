Project Spotlight

Featured Project: Skyline Apartment Complex

Flagship Project

Skyline Apartment Complex, Mumbai

A landmark residential development featuring 120 luxury units spread across 22 floors, complete with podium amenities, landscaped gardens, and state-of-the-art MEP systems. Our team delivered fully coordinated architectural, structural, MEP, and interior design drawings using BIM technology.

The project utilized ETABS for structural analysis, Revit for BIM coordination, and Lumion for photorealistic visualization, ensuring every detail was meticulously planned before construction began.
