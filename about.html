About OSM Design & Engineering Consultancy

Designing the Future with Innovation, Precision & Engineering Excellence

At OSM Design & Engineering Consultancy, we believe that every remarkable structure begins with a thoughtful design and a strong engineering foundation. For more than 10 years, we have been helping homeowners, developers, businesses, architects, contractors, and organizations transform ideas into functional, sustainable, and visually inspiring spaces.


Established with a vision to provide world-class design consultancy services, OSM has grown into a trusted multidisciplinary engineering firm serving clients across India, United Arab Emirates (UAE), Oman, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and the United Kingdom. Over the years, we have earned a reputation for delivering innovative, practical, and cost-effective design solutions tailored to the unique requirements of each project.


Our expertise extends beyond creating beautiful designs. We combine creativity with technical precision to ensure that every project is safe, efficient, compliant with applicable building regulations, and ready for successful construction.


Our Story

Bridging Creativity & Engineering Precision

OSM Design & Engineering Consultancy was founded with a simple objective — to bridge the gap between architectural creativity and engineering precision.


From our early projects to our current international portfolio, our philosophy has remained the same: design spaces that are functional, aesthetically appealing, structurally sound, and built for the future.


As the construction industry continues to evolve, we continuously invest in advanced design technologies, industry best practices, and skilled professionals to ensure that our clients receive modern engineering solutions that meet international quality standards.


Today, we proudly collaborate with private homeowners, real estate developers, construction companies, architects, consultants, industrial clients, educational institutions, healthcare organizations, and commercial businesses across multiple countries.

Integrated architecture, engineering, and visualization under one roof.
